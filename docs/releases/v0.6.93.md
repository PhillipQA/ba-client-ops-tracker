# v0.6.93 — Collapsible left menu, colour fixes and a bundled font

A layout and colour release. There is **no database migration and no new Google permission**, and nothing to change in Google Cloud or Render.

## Collapsible left menu

- Click the panel button beside the logo to shrink the menu to a strip of icons. The page gets the extra width (the menu goes from 252 px to 76 px). Click it again to open the menu.
- Hover an icon, or move to it with the keyboard, to see the module name. The unread count on Work Hub stays on its icon.
- The choice is remembered in the browser you used. It is not saved on your account.
- Screen readers still hear the module names, the current page is announced, and Escape closes a tooltip.
- Below 820 px wide the menu keeps its existing layout and the button is hidden.

## Colours

- **Light and Gray looks:** the signed-in card, template cards, Action Center filters and activity badges, the archive note and drop-downs no longer stay dark, and grey helper text is darker.
- **FSD and Test Case creators** follow the look you chose in the app, not the computer's setting. Document paper stays white.
- **Messages:** confirmations are blue and errors are red.
- **Default look:** primary buttons such as **Add task** are a clearer blue with white text.
- **Document Hub history** (Active and Archive tabs, Row and Tile switch, page size) is no longer white on the dark wallpaper looks.
- **Text size:** labels and badges that were 8 to 10 px are now 11 px. The text inside Gantt bars stays at 10 px.

## Font

Inter is now loaded from the app itself (Latin letters, weights 400, 500, 600 and 700, SIL Open Font License; about 110 KB in total, cached by the browser). Before this the app named Inter but never loaded it, so computers without Inter installed showed their own system font. Characters outside Western European languages still use the system font.

To try another face, tell us which one. It needs its font files added to `src/fonts` and one change to the `--font-ui` setting in `src/styles.css`, and every page follows.

## After the deploy

1. Open the public app URL, confirm **v0.6.93**, then hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) so the new styles load.
2. Click the panel button beside the logo. Confirm the menu collapses to icons, hover two or three icons and confirm the module name appears, then reload and confirm the menu stays collapsed.
3. Open **My Themes**, switch between Dark, Light and Gray, and open Action Center, Settings and Document Creation. Confirm no panel stays dark in the light looks.

Known limits: the collapsed menu is off on screens under 820 px wide, and the choice does not follow you to another browser.
