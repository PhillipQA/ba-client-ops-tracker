# v0.6.91 — Replies copy everyone on the email

**Reply by email** now works like Reply all. The sender gets the reply, and everyone else on the original email is added to Cc automatically, so you no longer type them in. No database change and no new Google permission.

- **Your own address is never added**, including an alias the email was delivered to, and nobody is listed twice.
- **One click to remove them.** The composer says how many people were copied and offers **Reply to sender only**. The Cc line stays editable.
- **Reply-To is honoured**, and replying to an email you wrote goes to the people you sent it to.
- **Works for earlier emails.** The recipients are read from Gmail when you click Reply. If Gmail cannot be reached, the reply opens with the sender only and a note asks you to add anyone else yourself.
- Blind-copy recipients of the original are not visible to you, so they are not added. The limit of 25 recipients per email still applies.

After the deploy, open the public app URL and confirm **v0.6.91**, then hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). To check it, open an email in Work Hub that was sent to several people, click **Reply by email**, and confirm the others appear in Cc and the note offers **Reply to sender only**.
