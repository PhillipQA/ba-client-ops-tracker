# v1.0.2 — Every Sheet row comes in, grouped into tasks and subtasks

**No database migration and no new setting.**

Before, a tracker connected to a Google Sheet matched rows by a key column and left out rows whose key was blank or repeated. Choosing a column such as Client could bring in only one row per client.

- **Every non-empty row comes into the tracker.**
- **Task name column** (first column by default): a row with a name is a task, and names may repeat. A row with that cell blank belongs to the task above it. The connect dialog shows how the rows will group.
- **The review is filled in:** named rows become new tasks, and the rows under them become their subtasks, including under a task an earlier sync already made. **Subtask title comes from** chooses the column for subtask names.
- Rows are recognised on later syncs by **name and order** (the 2nd "EGG" row, the 1st row under it). Inserting or sorting other rows is safe; inserting another row with the same name above an existing one shifts the later ones.
- **Already-connected trackers:** existing rows keep matching, and rows that were left out before come in on the next **Sync now**, ready to review.

After the deploy, confirm **v1.0.2**, hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R), open a tracker that follows a Sheet and click **Sync now**.
