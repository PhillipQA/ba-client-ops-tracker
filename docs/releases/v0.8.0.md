# v0.8.0 — Email signatures and templates

There is **one database migration**: apply [`database/migrations/schema-v26-email-templates.sql`](../../database/migrations/schema-v26-email-templates.sql) once (Supabase or Northflank PostgreSQL). Until it is applied, email works as before. No new Google permission.

- **Signature per person**, saved to your account and added to every new email, on any device.
- **Email templates**: pick one in New email by typing its name; the subject and message fill in above your signature.
- **Manage templates** (New email) or **Email templates** (Work Hub): create, view, edit, delete; **Save as template** from the email you are writing.
- **Shared** templates are for everyone on the account and anyone who sends email can change them; **Only me** templates are private.
- **Task or subtask** fields in New email, Save to Drive and DocHub are type-to-search, open work only.

After the deploy, apply schema v26, confirm **v0.8.0**, hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R), set your signature in New email and create a first template.
