# v0.6.94 — Trackers: Full view

There is **no database migration and no new Google permission**.

- **Full view:** in a tracker, click **Full view** next to Export CSV. The top bar (Add client, Add task and so on) is hidden, the left menu stays, and the grid fills the screen height. Click **Exit full view** to bring the top bar back. The choice is remembered in this browser; leaving the tracker always restores the top bar.
- **Spacing:** tracker pages no longer sit flush against the left menu, and the grid height fits the screen so the page does not scroll just to reach the bottom.

After the deploy, open the public app URL, confirm **v0.6.94**, hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R), open a tracker and try **Full view**.
