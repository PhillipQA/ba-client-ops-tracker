# v0.6.90 — Send document emails from the tracker

You can now send an email from the tracker, from your own Gmail, with files attached from Google Drive or your computer. No database change.

- **Open it where the document is:** **Email** on a file saved to a task or subtask, **Email signed PDF** on a fully signed document, **Reply by email** in Work Hub, or **New email** in Work Hub.
- **Undo for 10 seconds.** After Send, a bar counts down with **Undo** and **Send now**. Keep the page open until it is sent.
- **Linked to the work.** Every email is linked to a task or subtask. A copy (marked Sent) is kept in Work Hub, and a short note without the message text is added to the task's activity.
- **Safe to retry.** A double click or a dropped connection cannot send the same email twice.

**One-time setup:** add the scope `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send` under Google Auth Platform → Data Access in your Google Cloud project. Then each person who will send opens **Work Hub → My integration settings → Allow sending email** and approves it on Google's page.

After the deploy, open the public app URL and confirm **v0.6.90**, then hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). To check it, click **Allow sending email**, then open a task with a saved Drive file, click **Email**, send it to yourself, wait for the countdown and look in your Gmail Sent folder.
