# v0.6.95 — Trackers: freeze, resize and align columns

There is **no database migration and no new Google permission**.

Open a column's menu in a tracker:

- **Freeze column** keeps it in view while you scroll sideways. Frozen columns move to the front. The first column is frozen unless you unfreeze it, and frozen columns together are limited to about 700 px.
- **Width:** drag the edge of the header (double-click resets), or pick Narrow, Standard, Wide or Extra wide.
- **Align text:** Left, Center or Right.
- Long text cut off in a cell shows in full when you point at it.

These settings are saved with the tracker, so everyone sees the same layout, and only people who can edit the tracker can change them. Cells show one line; open the row for the whole value.

After the deploy, confirm **v0.6.95**, hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R), and try the column menu on a tracker with a long address column.
