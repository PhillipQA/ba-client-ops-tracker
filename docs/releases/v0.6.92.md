# v0.6.92 — Trackers, and recipient suggestions in the email composer and Document Creation

Two things in this release: a new **Trackers** page for sheets you shape yourself, and suggestions while you type in the To, Cc and Bcc lines (like Gmail) and in the signatory **Name** fields in Document Creation. Trackers needs one database migration (**schema v24**). There is no new Google permission, and nothing to change in Google Cloud or Render.

## Trackers

A sheet you design: your own columns, your own rows, saved views, and an optional link from a row to a task.

- **Start fast.** Create a tracker from a template (Site rollout, POS installation & BIR permits, Defect & enhancement log, Client ticket tracker) or from a blank one, or **Import a sheet** by pasting from Excel or Google Sheets or choosing a CSV file. The import shows what each column will become, flags password-like headings as Secret, and lists values it could not read instead of dropping them.
- **Columns you choose.** Text, long text, number, date, checkbox, select, multi-select, person and link. A column's type cannot be changed after it is made. Limits: 100 columns, 5,000 rows per tracker, 200 trackers per account.
- **Connected columns fill themselves.** Link a row to a task and **Task status**, **Task due date**, **Task owner**, **Sign-off status** (DocHub, including subtasks) and **Drive files** show the task's current values. Rows can also stand alone. A Git issue is a plain **Link** column.
- **Secret columns for access details.** Encrypted with the integration master key, masked on screen, left out of exports and of the row's history, and never sent with the rows. **Reveal** shows a value for 30 seconds and is recorded in the row's history; if the record cannot be saved, nothing is shown. Each Secret column is revealable by editors or by Administrators only. Viewers cannot reveal.
- **Filter, sort, group, save as a view.** Views appear as tabs. **Export CSV** downloads what is on screen, without Secret columns.
- **Safe to share.** Rows save one at a time. If two people edit the same row, the second is told and sees the current values. A tracker can be private to you, archived (read-only, restorable), and, once archived, deleted permanently.
- **Access.** Trackers is a new module. Administrators receive it after signing in again; other people need it assigned under **Users & Permissions → Module access** (new Contributors have it by default). Viewers can open trackers but not change them.

### After the deploy

1. Apply [`database/migrations/schema-v24-trackers.sql`](../../database/migrations/schema-v24-trackers.sql) once (Supabase or Northflank PostgreSQL). It also adds Trackers to every account's enabled modules. Until it is applied, the Trackers page says the migration is missing and nothing else changes.
2. Sign in again, open **Trackers**, start a tracker from a template and add a row.
3. Add a **Secret** column, save a fictional value, and confirm it shows as dots in the grid and only appears after **Reveal**. Open the row's **History** and confirm the reveal is listed without the value.
4. Link the row to a task and confirm the **Task status** column fills in.

Limits to know about: the grid has no board view, columns cannot be resized or dragged (use **Move left / Move right** in the column menu), long text is edited in the row panel, and visibility is the whole account or only you (no per-person sharing). Connected columns read from tasks only.

## Recipient suggestions

The To, Cc and Bcc lines suggest people while you type, and the signatory **Name** fields in Document Creation suggest the matching email.

- **Who is suggested:** people you have emailed (from your Gmail Sent folder, most emailed first), people who emailed you, your clients' email addresses and your active teammates. Each suggestion shows where it came from.
- **How to use it:** type part of a name or address, then use the arrow keys with Enter or Tab, or click. Pasting a list works as before, and typing in the middle of a line replaces only the person under the cursor.
- **Left out:** no-reply and other automatic senders, and anyone already on the email.
- **Private to you.** The list comes from your own mailbox, is kept only in server memory for 10 minutes, is never saved in the database or shown to others, and is dropped when you disconnect Gmail.
- **Signatory emails in Document Creation.** Choose a person in a DRF or Sign Off **Name** field and their email is filled in. A full name that is exactly one known person fills an empty email when you leave the field, with a note to check it. No match means the email stays empty, and a name with two different addresses is never guessed. An email you typed is never replaced, and a filled-in email is removed again if you change the name.
- **Limits:** the latest 120 sent emails and the last 500 saved emails are read. Google Contacts and the company directory are not included.

After the deploy, open the public app URL and confirm **v0.6.92**, then hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). To check recipient suggestions, open **New email**, type the first letters of someone you have emailed before, and confirm they appear with how often you emailed them. Then open **Document Creation → DRF → Signatories**, type the name of a teammate or client contact in **Name**, and confirm their email appears in **Email (DocHub only)**.
