# v0.6.92 — Recipient suggestions in the email composer and Document Creation

The To, Cc and Bcc lines now suggest people while you type, like Gmail, and the signatory **Name** fields in Document Creation suggest the matching email. No database change and no new Google permission.

- **Who is suggested:** people you have emailed (from your Gmail Sent folder, most emailed first), people who emailed you, your clients' email addresses and your active teammates. Each suggestion shows where it came from.
- **How to use it:** type part of a name or address, then use the arrow keys with Enter or Tab, or click. Pasting a list works as before, and typing in the middle of a line replaces only the person under the cursor.
- **Left out:** no-reply and other automatic senders, and anyone already on the email.
- **Private to you.** The list comes from your own mailbox, is kept only in server memory for 10 minutes, is never saved in the database or shown to others, and is dropped when you disconnect Gmail.
- **Signatory emails in Document Creation.** Choose a person in a DRF or Sign Off **Name** field and their email is filled in. A full name that is exactly one known person fills an empty email when you leave the field, with a note to check it. No match means the email stays empty, and a name with two different addresses is never guessed. An email you typed is never replaced, and a filled-in email is removed again if you change the name.
- **Limits:** the latest 120 sent emails and the last 500 saved emails are read. Google Contacts and the company directory are not included.

After the deploy, open the public app URL and confirm **v0.6.92**, then hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). To check it, open **New email**, type the first letters of someone you have emailed before, and confirm they appear with how often you emailed them. Then open **Document Creation → DRF → Signatories**, type the name of a teammate or client contact in **Name**, and confirm their email appears in **Email (DocHub only)**.
